LambdaVim

A minimal, modern Neovim configuration designed for developers who want a clean and productive editing experience without the bloat.

Project Goals

Most Neovim distros come with a ton of bloat, hide plugins, and force you to use their configuration strictly. LambdaVim's goal is to give the user full control over their own Neovim configuration while providing a simple, bloat-free and intuitive starting configuration. This also means LambdaVim is very beginner friendly!
